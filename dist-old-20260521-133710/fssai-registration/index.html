FSSAI Registration

FSSAI Registration for Food Businesses

Get your food business registered or licensed with professional document and application support.

Apply for FSSAI Check License Type

Overview

Every food business operator in India is required to obtain registration or license under FSSAI, depending on business type, scale, turnover, and activity. FSSAI registrations and licenses are handled through the FoSCoS portal.

We help food businesses choose the correct FSSAI category and complete the application process with proper documentation.

Who Needs This Service

  • Home-based food sellers
  • Cloud kitchens
  • Restaurants and cafes
  • Food manufacturers
  • Food traders
  • Food distributors
  • Food importers/exporters
  • Online food sellers
  • Packaged food businesses

What We Do

  • Basic FSSAI registration
  • State FSSAI license
  • Central FSSAI license
  • Document checklist support
  • Application preparation
  • FoSCoS filing support
  • Clarification support
  • Renewal/modification support

Documents Required

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Photograph
  • Business address proof
  • Food business details
  • Nature of food activity
  • Rent agreement / ownership proof
  • NOC, if applicable
  • Business registration proof, if applicable
  • Food safety management documents, if applicable
  • Manufacturing details, if applicable

Process

  1. 1 Business activity review
  2. 2 License type identification
  3. 3 Document collection
  4. 4 Application preparation
  5. 5 Filing through FoSCoS
  6. 6 Department clarification support, if any
  7. 7 Certificate/license shared

FAQs

Which FSSAI license do I need?

It depends on turnover, business activity, location, and scale of operation.

Is FSSAI required for home food business?

Yes, food businesses generally require registration or license depending on applicability.

Can you help with FSSAI renewal?

Yes. We assist with renewal, modification, and new applications.

Is inspection required?

Inspection may be required depending on the license category and department process.
