Overview

Government e-Marketplace, commonly known as GeM, allows sellers and service providers to register, list products or services, participate in government procurement opportunities, and expand reach to government buyers.

The GeM seller process includes verifying the primary user’s credentials, completing sign-up, and registering business details.

  • Business profile setup guidance
  • Document checklist support
  • Product/service category guidance
  • Basic listing support
  • Profile correction support

Documents Required

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile number
  • Business registration proof
  • GST certificate, if applicable
  • MSME/Udyam certificate, if available
  • Bank details
  • Email ID and mobile number
  • Authorized person details
  • Product/service details
  • Business address proof
