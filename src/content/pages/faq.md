## GST Services FAQs

## 9. Who needs GST registration?

GST registration may be required for businesses crossing the applicable turnover threshold, e-commerce sellers, inter-state suppliers and other categories specified under GST law. Applicability depends on business type, location and nature of supply.

---

## 10. What documents are required for GST registration?

## 12. Can you help with GST notices?

## 13. Can GST registration be cancelled?

## Company Registration FAQs

## 14. Which is better: Private Limited Company, LLP or Proprietorship?

There is no single answer for every business. A Private Limited Company may be suitable for scalable startups and funding-oriented businesses. LLP may be suitable for professional or partnership-based businesses. Proprietorship may suit very small individual businesses. The right structure depends on liability, taxation, compliance cost, ownership and future plans.

---

## 15. Do you help with Private Limited Company registration?

Yes. We assist with name approval, DSC, DIN-related process, incorporation forms, MOA, AOA and other documents required for company registration.

---

## 16. Do you help with LLP registration?

Yes. We assist with LLP registration, name approval, incorporation filing and LLP agreement-related support.

---

## 17. What is OPC registration?

OPC means One Person Company. It is a company structure suitable for a single founder who wants a corporate form with limited liability, subject to applicable rules.

---

## 18. What happens after company registration?

After registration, companies may need post-incorporation compliance such as bank account opening support, commencement of business filing, statutory registers, auditor appointment, GST registration if applicable and annual ROC filings.

---

## FSSAI FAQs

## 23. Who needs FSSAI registration or license?

Food business operators such as food manufacturers, traders, restaurants, cloud kitchens, food sellers, distributors and related businesses may require FSSAI registration or license depending on business type and turnover.

---

## 24. What is the difference between Basic, State and Central FSSAI License?

The type of FSSAI registration or license depends on factors such as turnover, business size, location and nature of food activity. Basic registration is generally for small food businesses, while State and Central licenses apply to larger or specific categories of food businesses.

---

## 25. Do you help with FSSAI renewal?

Yes. We assist with FSSAI registration, license application, renewal and modification support.

## 26. What is Startup India registration?

Startup India registration helps eligible startups obtain recognition under the Startup India initiative, subject to eligibility conditions and required documents.

MSME/Udyam registration is used by eligible micro, small and medium enterprises to obtain recognition as an MSME.
