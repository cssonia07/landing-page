Overview

A One Person Company is suitable for a single entrepreneur who wants to run a business with a company structure and limited liability protection, without bringing in another shareholder as a business partner.

OPC registration is part of company incorporation-related work handled through MCA-linked corporate registration systems.

Who Should Choose OPC

  • Solo founders
  • Individual consultants
  • Small business owners
  • Professionals starting independently
  • Founders who want limited liability
  • Businesses wanting a formal corporate identity

What We Do

  • OPC suitability consultation
  • Name selection guidance
  • DSC support
  • Director and nominee documentation
  • Incorporation filing support
  • MOA and AOA support
  • PAN/TAN support
  • Post-incorporation compliance guidance

Documents Required

  • PAN card of director
  • Aadhaar card of director
  • Photograph
  • Email ID and mobile number
  • Nominee details
  • Nominee consent
  • Registered office proof
  • Electricity bill
  • Rent agreement / ownership proof
  • NOC from owner
  • Business activity details
  • Proposed company names

FAQs

Can one person start a company in India?

Yes. OPC allows a single person to start a company subject to eligibility conditions.

Is nominee required for OPC?

Yes. Nominee details and consent are required.

Can OPC be converted later?

Yes, conversion may be possible subject to applicable legal provisions.

Does OPC need ROC filing?

Yes. OPC also has annual compliance requirements.
