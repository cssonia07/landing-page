GST Services FAQs

Who needs GST registration?

GST registration may be required for businesses crossing the applicable turnover threshold, e-commerce sellers, inter-state suppliers and other categories specified under GST law. Applicability depends on business type, location and nature of supply.

What documents are required for GST registration?

Common documents include PAN, Aadhaar, photograph, business address proof, bank details, electricity bill, rent agreement or ownership proof, and authorization documents depending on entity type.

Do you help with GST return filing?

Yes. We assist with GST return filing for businesses, traders, service providers and other GST-registered persons.

Can you help with GST notices?

Yes. We can review GST notices, explain the issue, prepare response documents and assist with reply submission depending on the notice type.

Can GST registration be cancelled?

Yes, GST registration can be cancelled where applicable, subject to GST rules, pending returns, liabilities and departmental requirements.

Company Registration FAQs

Which is better: Private Limited Company, LLP or Proprietorship?

There is no single answer for every business. A Private Limited Company may be suitable for scalable startups and funding-oriented businesses. LLP may be suitable for professional or partnership-based businesses. Proprietorship may suit very small individual businesses. The right structure depends on liability, taxation, compliance cost, ownership and future plans.

Do you help with Private Limited Company registration?

Yes. We assist with name approval, DSC, DIN-related process, incorporation forms, MOA, AOA and other documents required for company registration.

Do you help with LLP registration?

Yes. We assist with LLP registration, name approval, incorporation filing and LLP agreement-related support.

What is OPC registration?

OPC means One Person Company. It is a company structure suitable for a single founder who wants a corporate form with limited liability, subject to applicable rules.

What happens after company registration?

After registration, companies may need post-incorporation compliance such as bank account opening support, commencement of business filing, statutory registers, auditor appointment, GST registration if applicable and annual ROC filings.

Income Tax FAQs

Do you file income tax returns?

Yes. We assist with income tax return filing for individuals, professionals, freelancers and businesses.

What documents are required for ITR filing?

Common documents include PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16, bank statements, investment proofs, capital gain details, business income details, GST data where applicable and other income-related records.

Can you help with tax notices?

Yes. We assist with reviewing income tax notices and preparing suitable replies based on available documents and facts.

Do freelancers need to file income tax returns?

Yes, freelancers may need to file income tax returns depending on income, deductions, tax liability and applicable provisions. They may also need GST registration depending on services and turnover.

FSSAI FAQs

Who needs FSSAI registration or license?

Food business operators such as food manufacturers, traders, restaurants, cloud kitchens, food sellers, distributors and related businesses may require FSSAI registration or license depending on business type and turnover.

What is the difference between Basic, State and Central FSSAI License?

The type of FSSAI registration or license depends on factors such as turnover, business size, location and nature of food activity. Basic registration is generally for small food businesses, while State and Central licenses apply to larger or specific categories of food businesses.

Do you help with FSSAI renewal?

Yes. We assist with FSSAI registration, license application, renewal and modification support.

Startup & Business Registration FAQs

What is Startup India registration?

Startup India registration helps eligible startups obtain recognition under the Startup India initiative, subject to eligibility conditions and required documents.

What is MSME/Udyam registration?

MSME/Udyam registration is used by eligible micro, small and medium enterprises to obtain recognition as an MSME.

What is GeM registration?

GeM registration helps eligible sellers and service providers register on the Government e-Marketplace to sell goods or services to government buyers.

What is IEC registration?

IEC stands for Import Export Code. It is generally required for businesses involved in import or export of goods or services, subject to applicable rules.

ROC and Annual Compliance FAQs

Do companies need annual compliance even if there is no business activity?

Yes. Registered companies and LLPs may still have annual filing obligations even if there is no business activity, unless legally closed or exempted under applicable law.

What happens if ROC filings are delayed?

Delay in ROC filings may lead to additional fees, penalties and compliance consequences. It is better to complete annual filings on time.

Do you provide annual compliance packages?

Yes. We provide annual compliance and filing support for companies, LLPs and other business entities.

FEMA Compliance FAQs

Do you provide FEMA compliance support?

Yes. We assist with FEMA-related documentation and compliance guidance for eligible transactions and business requirements.

Who may need FEMA compliance?

Businesses receiving foreign investment, making overseas investment, issuing shares to non-residents or handling cross-border transactions may require FEMA-related compliance support.
