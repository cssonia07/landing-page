Overview

GST notices can be issued for return mismatches, late filings, input tax credit issues, registration matters, non-compliance, or department queries. The right response depends on the facts, notice type, documents, and deadline.

NiyamDesk helps you understand the notice in simple language, collect the right records, prepare a suitable reply, and manage follow-up where applicable.

Who Needs This Service

  • Businesses that received a GST notice
  • Taxpayers with return mismatches
  • Businesses facing input tax credit queries
  • GST-registered freelancers and professionals
  • E-commerce sellers
  • Businesses with delayed or missed GST filings
  • Taxpayers needing clarification reply support

What We Do

  • GST notice review
  • Issue and deadline explanation
  • Document checklist preparation
  • Reply drafting support
  • Supporting document organization
  • GST portal submission guidance
  • Follow-up support where applicable

Documents Required

  • Copy of GST notice
  • GST login details, if portal review is required
  • GST returns and ledgers
  • Sales and purchase invoices
  • E-way bills, if applicable
  • Bank statements, if required
  • Books of accounts or summaries
  • Earlier correspondence with the department, if any
