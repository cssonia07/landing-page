Overview

Startup India recognition is provided under DPIIT for eligible startups. Recognised startups may access benefits such as easier compliance, IPR fast-tracking, and other startup ecosystem benefits, subject to eligibility and applicable rules.

We help startups understand eligibility, prepare documents, and submit the recognition application properly.

FAQs

Can any business apply for Startup India recognition?

No. The business must meet eligibility requirements under Startup India/DPIIT.

Is Private Limited Company compulsory?

Eligible entities may include Private Limited Companies, LLPs, and registered partnership firms, subject to current DPIIT criteria.

Do you help prepare the startup write-up?

Yes. We help prepare clear business description content for the application.

Does recognition guarantee tax exemption?

No. Recognition and tax benefits are separate and subject to specific eligibility and approval conditions.
