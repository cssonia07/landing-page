Overview

Companies and LLPs must complete statutory filings and maintain proper compliance records after registration. ROC compliance usually includes financial statement filing, annual returns, director-related compliance, and event-based filings depending on the entity type.

MCA-linked services support company/LLP registration, name availability, and corporate compliance processes.

Who Needs This Service

  • Private Limited Companies
  • OPCs
  • LLPs
  • Section 8 companies
  • Companies with pending annual filing
  • Inactive companies needing regularization
  • Startups after incorporation
  • Businesses wanting compliance calendar support

What We Do

  • Annual ROC filing support
  • AOC-4 filing support
  • MGT-7 / MGT-7A support
  • LLP annual filing support
  • Director KYC support
  • Event-based filing support
  • Minutes and documentation support
  • Compliance calendar support
  • Penalty and pending filing review
  • MCA status review

Documents Required

  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • MOA/AOA or LLP agreement
  • PAN and TAN
  • Financial statements
  • Audit report, if applicable
  • Board meeting details
  • Shareholding details
  • Director/partner details
  • DSC of authorized person
  • Previous filing records
  • Bank statements and books data, if required

Process

  1. 1 Entity compliance status review
  2. 2 Pending forms and due dates are identified

  4. 4 Forms are prepared

FAQs

Is ROC filing required every year?

Yes. Registered companies and LLPs generally have annual filing obligations.

What happens if annual filing is delayed?

Delay may lead to additional fees, penalties, and compliance complications.

Do you handle pending ROC filings?

Yes. We review pending status and help regularize filings.

Do you provide ongoing compliance support?

Yes. Monthly and annual compliance management plans are available.
