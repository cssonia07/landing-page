Overview

A Private Limited Company is a popular business structure for startups and growing businesses because it offers a separate legal identity, organized ownership structure, and better credibility with investors, banks, vendors, and customers.

Company and LLP name availability checks and registration-related services are facilitated through MCA-linked online services.

Who Should Choose a Private Limited Company

  • Startups planning to scale
  • Businesses seeking investor funding
  • Businesses with two or more promoters
  • Service companies
  • Technology businesses
  • E-commerce businesses
  • Agencies and consulting firms
  • Businesses wanting a formal corporate structure

What We Do

  • Name availability guidance
  • DSC support
  • DIN-related assistance
  • Drafting basic incorporation documents
  • SPICe+ filing support
  • PAN and TAN application support
  • MOA and AOA preparation support
  • Certificate of Incorporation follow-up
  • Post-incorporation compliance guidance

Documents Required

  • PAN card of directors
  • Aadhaar card of directors
  • Photograph of directors
  • Email ID and mobile number
  • Business address proof
  • Electricity bill
  • Rent agreement / ownership proof
  • NOC from owner
  • Proposed company names
  • Business activity details
  • Shareholding details

FAQs

How many people are required to register a Private Limited Company?

A Private Limited Company generally requires at least two directors and two shareholders.

Is office address required?

Yes. A registered office address is required.

Can my home address be used as registered office?

Yes, subject to proper address proof and NOC.

Do you also handle annual compliance after registration?

Yes. We provide annual ROC filing and ongoing compliance support.
