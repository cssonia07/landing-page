Overview

GST registration is important for businesses that are required to collect GST, claim input tax credit, sell goods or services across states, sell through e-commerce platforms, or meet turnover-based GST applicability requirements.

We help you complete the GST registration process in a simple and structured manner by reviewing your documents, preparing the application, submitting it on the GST portal, and assisting with department queries where required. GST registration applications are made through the GST portal under the registration section.

FAQs

Is GST registration compulsory for every business?

No. GST registration depends on turnover, business activity, place of supply, e-commerce involvement, and other GST provisions.

Can I apply for GST online?

Yes. GST registration is applied online through the GST portal.

How long does GST registration take?

It depends on document accuracy and department processing. We share an estimated timeline after reviewing your documents.

Can you help if GST application is rejected or sent for clarification?

Yes. We assist with clarification and re-submission support.
